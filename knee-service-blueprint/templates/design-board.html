Viewer and temporary workspace · Updated 2026-10-10

Overview = patient-specific MRI Volume + three linked MPR planes from one selected source series. Direction tabs = original acquired stacks; Images / Series = acquisition browser.

Open workspace without account → upload/view studies scoped to this temporary browser session → Clear session or wait for expiry. Shared example remains read-only. See Temporary session and upload limits.

MRI Volume uses DICOM voxel intensity, not generic anatomy or segmentation. Its transparent three-orbit gizmo shows horizontal, vertical, and diagonal camera paths; arrowheads mark movement direction. These lines are not slice planes. See viewer specification. Older mockup below is historical.

Nên làm tính năng nào trước?

  1. D1–3: Luồng chạy được. Docker → Upload/index DICOM → Mở một series → Lướt lát.
  2. D4: Examples. Seed có version/checksum; mở mẫu từ danh sách.
  3. D5–6: Workspace tree và display. Cây Study → Series → slice, 4 ô Overview, focused direction tabs, wheel/pinch zoom, drag-pan, Contrast/Brightness/Invert/Reset và Analyze placeholder.
  4. D7–10: Persistence và bàn giao. Xóa study upload, restart, QA, README local; model AI/Triton để phase sau.

MPR/volume yêu cầu geometry phù hợp; study không đủ điều kiện vẫn xem được native acquisition stacks. Đây là research viewer, chưa được thẩm định lâm sàng.
